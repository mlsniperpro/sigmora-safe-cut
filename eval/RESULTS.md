# Results

Generated by `eval/report.py` from the committed runs in `eval/results/` only; every figure links to its run.
Recall: the share of should-hide entities covered (≥ 95 % of the box) on at least 95 % of their visible frames. Exposure: the share of should-hide entity-frames under 90 % covered. Leak bursts: runs of ≥ 3 consecutive exposed frames. Over-blur: the share of should-keep entities hidden (≥ 50 % covered) on more than 10 % of their frames. Metrics and their tests: `eval/metrics.py`, `tests/test_metrics.py`.

## Service limits (from `safecut/config.py` defaults)

- Per-clip spend cap (`SAFECUT_JOB_USD_CAP`): $3.00.
- Daily cap for judge and public codes (`SAFECUT_DAILY_USD_CAP`): $3.00, reset at 00:00 UTC.
- Pilot codes: $6.00 each, $20.00 in total; the recording code: up to $15.00.
- A job's worst-case estimate before it starts: the larger of $1.12 and $2.24 per minute of footage.
- Clips: up to 60 s and 150 MB; jobs are deleted 24 h after they're created (the deletion sweep runs every 15 min, and a job past its time is refused on sight).
