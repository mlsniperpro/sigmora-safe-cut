# Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon — official participant guide (captured 2026-10-26 16:25Z)

Source: cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/details (login-gated; transcribed from the owner's signed-in tab).

## Problem statements (project must build toward one)
- **Build for Your World**: bring Meta's models into your domain. Solve a problem you know firsthand, free up time and mental space, and make something possible that wasn't before.
- **Push the Frontier**: use Meta's models on challenges in software, robotics, manufacturing, energy, materials and beyond. Give a machine a new capability, speed up a scientific experiment, or find a better way to understand and control a physical system.

The project must use Meta's models: the Muse Spark series, Muse Wonder, Muse Image, Muse Voice Transcribe and SAM 3.1 through the Meta Model API; the open-weight models Muse Glimmer and Muse Spark Open; and the Muse Code coding agent.

## Rules
- Repositories must be public. Teams are at most 4 people; solo is allowed.
- **The demo must show only features, code and functionality built during the Hackathon.** Judges must be able to tell what was created during the event; if they can't, the team is disqualified.
- **New work only**: everything presented must be built during the event. Startups may build on existing products, but must clearly identify what is new.
- Banned: anything that violates legal, ethical or platform policies, and any code, data or assets you don't have the rights to.
- Anti-projects to avoid:
  - AI that generates or gives medical advice (for example a mental-health advisor or a skin-blemish detector)
  - basic RAG
  - simple image analyzers
  - simple "AI for Education" chatbots
  - AI job-application screeners
  - AI nutrition coaches
  - personality analyzers
  - sports analyzers or coaches
  - anything political or involving war

## Submission
- Teams and startups submit one project, entered by one Representative.
- Required: a **three-minute demo video**, a public repo and a live product.
- Before submitting, check the repo is public, the video link works, and every contributor is listed.

## Judging
1. **Stage 1 (pass/fail):** the project fits a theme and reasonably uses the Meta Model API and/or Meta open-weight models.
2. **Stage 2 (each criterion scored 0–10):**

   | Criterion | Weight | What judges ask |
   |---|---|---|
   | Impact | 30% | Real-world potential, who benefits, could it become a product, does it fit a problem statement |
   | Use of Meta AI tooling | 25% | Creative use beyond a basic integration; capabilities that surprise them |
   | Technical depth & execution | 20% | Sound, refined engineering with real craft |
   | Demo | 25% | Working and impressive, holds up live, a video that is genuinely cool to watch |

3. **Stage 3:** the top 5 per track go to a final round, where judges watch the demo videos live and rank them.
