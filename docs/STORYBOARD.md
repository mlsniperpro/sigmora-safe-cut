# Safe Cut: demo video storyboard (2:50)

Status: **v1, 2026-10-24 (PRE-B, docs only).** Shot by shot for the beats in [DEMO.md](DEMO.md). The screens are specified in [UI-SPEC.md](UI-SPEC.md); the clips are the hero clips in [SHOTLIST.md](SHOTLIST.md); the numbers come from WP7's `eval/RESULTS.md` ([EVAL.md](EVAL.md), [WP7](work-packages/WP7.md)). The pipeline the VO describes is [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) §2 (Set-of-Mark role calls §2.3, re-entry policy §2.5, audit §2.8). WP8 turns this into the final `docs/VIDEO-SCRIPT.md` on Nov 3.

## Rules for this video

1. **Numbers are placeholders** like `{recall_full}` until WP8 fills them from a committed `eval/results/<run-id>/metrics.json` ([AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) rule 9). If a number is weak, it's shown anyway ([DEMO.md](DEMO.md)). If a placeholder has no committed source by Nov 3, the shot changes; nobody types a number in.
2. **Nothing staged as real.** Every screen shot is a recording of the deployed app. If a shot uses replayed data, the demo-mode banner is visible in it ([UI-SPEC.md](UI-SPEC.md) §6). If something the script expects didn't happen on the real run (e.g. the audit found no leak), the script changes to what did happen.
3. **Only consenting people on screen.** Hero clips only (signed releases), fake PII on every screen and document, no real keys, emails or private footage ([SHOTLIST.md](SHOTLIST.md), [DEMO.md](DEMO.md) Production).
4. **The café menu is shown in Needs you, not Kept clear.** Under the policy ([DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) §2.5, WP3), a harmless screen goes to Needs you and is never kept clear automatically, so beat 5 shows the real behaviour: Muse Spark reads it as a menu, then the creator confirms with one tap. That's stronger on screen than an automatic keep, because it shows reasoning and human control together. [DEMO.md](DEMO.md) beat 5 says "clear"; this storyboard supersedes that line.
5. **Baselines are labelled as ours.** "Blur every face" is our implementation of that approach (WP7's `blur_all`), labelled "Blur every face (baseline)". Don't say it's YouTube's or CapCut's actual output.
6. **Captions on** for the whole video. **Decision: the VO is the owner's own recorded voice (O9), not the ElevenLabs clone.** That leaves nothing AI-generated to label or disclose, and if a rule asks about synthetic voices the answer is already no. VO at about 150 words a minute; the VO below is about 265 words (counted per shot), and no shot runs faster than 150 wpm.
7. **No literature numbers on screen.** [DEMO.md](DEMO.md) beat 7's "human-agreement ceiling line" (EVAL.md §1's 80–93% from Hasan et al. 2020) is superseded: shot 7.1 shows only our own label agreement from `{kappa}`, because every number in the video must come from a committed `metrics.json` ([AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) rule 9). The paper can be cited in the README's methods text, never as a line on our chart. Updating DEMO.md beat 7 is a request to its owner (in the PRE-B PR).

**Sources key.** `H1A`/`H1B` = hero clip 1 "street interview", take A / take B (the van re-entry); `H2` = hero 2 "café"; `H3` = hero 3 "walk-through". `R-xx` = a render; `SR-xx` = a screen recording (§3); `CH-xx` = a chart from `eval/report.py`; `GFX` = a title card made in the editor.

## 3. Screen recordings to capture on Nov 3

**Setup for every recording:** the deployed app on the custom domain (not localhost, not a preview URL); a fresh Chrome profile with no extensions, bookmarks or notifications; window content exactly 1920×1080 at 100% zoom; cursor visible; 30 fps; system audio off (VO is added later). Use the hero jobs run **live** on Nov 3 where possible; if the API is down and replay is used, the demo-mode banner is visible and stays in the edit. **Takes:** recordings that re-open a finished job (`SR-02`–`SR-07`, `SR-09`, `SR-11`) cost nothing, so record each of them at least twice. **Order matters, because an edit after a render needs a paid re-render** (UI-SPEC §3.5 row 6): record `SR-01`, then start the H1B and H2 live runs (not recorded; each owner timed review follows its "Ready for you", BLOCKED-PRE-B), then every take of `SR-02` and `SR-03` (they make no edits), then the `SR-04` takes, pressing `Ctrl+Z` off camera (focus on a chip) before each later take so it starts from the Needs-you state again (free: nothing is rendered; UI-SPEC §10.5 undo stack), with the last take ending on the same decisions as the owner's timed review (BLOCKED-PRE-B: SR-04's action follows that review's decision, and its other edits are re-made off camera before `SR-08`); then `SR-05` the same way on H2 (`Ctrl+Z` between takes), and `SR-06` (H1B, no edits; H1B's render `R-03B` follows it, from the decisions of H1B's timed review), then `SR-08` (H1A's render), and only then `SR-07`, `SR-09` and `SR-11`. No edit is made on H1A after `SR-08`. The paid ones are recorded once, with at most one retake between them.

**Budget (PRE-A owns the figures, `docs/API-NOTES.md` §9.2 @ `4bb3cec`):** paid work starts only at `SR-01` (H1A's upload → review), the H1B and H2 live runs (started right after `SR-01`, uploads not recorded), H1B's render `R-03B`, and `SR-08` (H1A's render); every recording re-opens one of those jobs. The paid runs on Nov 3 are H1A, H1B and H2 once each, plus the `SR-08` render and H1B's render (`R-03B`), and one retake of `SR-01` or `SR-08` if one fails. PRE-A prices exactly this against a **$12.00 hard cap**: 3 runs at most $1.99 each (its 60 s worst case) + 2 renders + the retake. They run on the **owner recording code** (UI-SPEC §8 request 20): $12 in total, not per day, outside the $3/day public cap (so recording never locks judges out), valid until Nov 3 23:59 UTC, and revoked after the recordings. If the $12 runs out, the owner may raise the code's `cap_usd` in the `SAFECUT_ACCESS_CODES` deploy secret to **at most $14.86** (+$2.86). That uses PRE-A's whole $2.86 headroom against the $150 credits, shared with every other overrun, and nothing beyond it ever fits, so a failure after that means recording with what has already run. A retake never moves onto the public cap.
